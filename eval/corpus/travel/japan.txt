tokyo packing list (flight Oct 3 to 16)

clothes
- 5 days of shirts (2 long sleeve, 3 short). merino is best, no wrinkles
- 2 pairs jeans or chinos
- 1 nicer outfit for the ryokan dinner
- underwear and socks x 8, a few extra for Hakone
- warm layer: the fleece and the packable down jacket. Hakone gets cold at night in October
- rain jacket with a hood. Umbrella too, the cheap ones are sold at every convenience store anyway

shoes
- walking shoes you've already broken in. Tokyo is 15,000 steps some days
- slip-ons for the ryokan and the temples, easy to take off

tech
- passport (must be valid 6 months past the exit date)
- JR pass voucher printed out
- phone, charger, 1 power bank
- type A adapter. The phone chargers are fine on 100V, the hair dryer is not
- Suica on the phone (Apple Wallet)
- pocket wifi, pick it up at the Narita counter on arrival

health
- prescriptions in original bottles, 2 weeks extra
- ibuprofen, band-aids. Motion sickness tablets probably not needed, nobody got sick last time
- hand sanitizer (the wet towels they hand out are better)

misc
- small coin purse, lots of small shops are cash only
- reusable shopping bag
- notebook, translation app downloaded offline
- gifts for the host family in Shinjuku: the Boston chocolate box and two tins of tea

Don't forget: the umbrella goes in the carry-on, not the checked bag. Bags got misrouted last time. Narita to Shinjuku on the N'EX takes about 80 min.
