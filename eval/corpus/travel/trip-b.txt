porto weekend, april 2022 (2 nights, just the two of us)

Stayed at a small guesthouse near Bolhao market, room 4 with the blue tiles and a very loud pigeon on the sill. Good breakfast, bad wifi.

Friday: flew in via Lisbon and took the bus from the airport. Walked down to Ribeira in the afternoon. The river is prettier than the photos. Dinner at a cheap place on Rua das Flores, octopus rice.

Saturday: Douro valley boat tour, 3 hours, run by Rabelo Tours. Stopped at a quinta for tasting. The 10-year tawny was excellent and 40 euro a bottle, so we bought one. Afternoon: Livraria Lello (crowded, 15 euro ticket that goes toward a book), then the Clerigos tower, climb it, 240 steps. Dinner at a tapas place with a live guitarist.

Sunday: Serralves museum and gardens, coffee, then the airport.

Notes: pastries at Cafe Majestic are overpriced. The cheaper bakery on Rua de Santa Catarina was better. Airport bus is 5 euro, a cab is 25 or more. Bring a jacket for the boat.

We'd go back. Lisbon was more exciting, Porto was more relaxed. Next time: the Douro in the fall for the harvest.
