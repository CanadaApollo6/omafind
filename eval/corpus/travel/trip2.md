# Lisbon - 4 days (Mar 14-18)

Flight: TAP from Boston, arrive 7:20am on the 14th. Hotel: Casa do Miradouro in Alfama. Check-in is 3pm but they'll hold bags from 10 if we ask at the desk.

Day 1 (Thu): walk up through Alfama to the Sao Jorge castle. Buy tickets online, the line is huge after 11. Lunch at Tasca do Chico for the bifana. Evening: fado at Clube de Fado, book ahead, 8pm show, cover is 25 euro each.

Day 2 (Fri): tram 28 early (7:30-8am) before the crowds, ride it all the way to Graca. Sintra day trip by train from Rossio, about 40 min. Pena Palace is worth it but book the 9:30 entry. Pasteis de Belem for the original custard tarts. Back by 6 for dinner at Taberna da Rua das Flores.

Day 3 (Sat): Time Out Market in the morning, then Belem tower and the Jeronimos monastery. Afternoon free. Port tasting at a bar near Cais do Sodre. Ruth wants the white port.

Day 4 (Sun): LX Factory for brunch and shopping, then the ferry across the Tagus to Cacilhas. Last dinner: grilled sardines back in Alfama, Ruth says it's the best meal of the trip.

Budget: about 1,400 for hotel and flights per couple (not counting the flights themselves). Cash in euros, most places take cards. Metro card is Viva Viagem, buy at the station, 0.50 deposit.

Packing: comfortable shoes for the hills (the cobblestones are brutal), a light rain jacket, power adapter type C/F. Leave the big suitcase at home, the hills and the trams are not kind to it.

Confirm by March 1: fado booking, Pena Palace tickets, Taberna. Email the host about the early bag drop.
