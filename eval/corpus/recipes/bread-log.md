# starter log - the jar (named Gerald, don't ask)

Starter: 100% whole wheat, fed 1:1:1 by weight (starter:flour:water). Kitchen runs about 72F in winter, 78F in summer.

Summer schedule:
- 7am: discard all but 30g, feed 30g flour + 30g water
- 1pm: check. If it has doubled and domed, it's ready. Usually 4 to 6 hours.
- if it has to sit overnight, feed at 10pm with 10g flour + 10g water to slow it down

Winter schedule: fridge storage. Take it out Friday night, feed twice Saturday (8am and 2pm), bake Sunday morning. Back in the fridge after the last feed of the week.

Recent bakes:
- Oct 6: 75% hydration, 500g flour, 375g water, 100g starter. Gummy inside, bulk ferment was 30 min short. Next time go 5.5 hours at 76F.
- Oct 12: same recipe, bulk 5h15. Great oven spring. Scored deeper across the top with a razor. Crumb more open.
- Oct 13: tried 20% spelt. Nutty smell, darker crust. Bottom got too dark at 450 for the last 10 min. Drop to 425.

Things to fix:
- Gerald smells like nail polish remover on day 3 of being neglected. Feed him before a weekend away, not after.
- kitchen thermometer reads 3 degrees off. Use the oven-light trick to find the warm spot.
- buy a bigger banneton, the 9-inch one is too small for 500g of dough.

Starter is about 4 years old, from Neil at the Saturday market. He says it came from a bakery in Brooklyn that closed in 2019.
