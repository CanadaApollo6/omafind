lentil soup, big pot

Makes about 3 quarts, enough for the week plus one lunch for Dev.

ingredients
1 cup brown or green lentils, rinsed
1 yellow onion, diced
2 carrots, diced
2 celery stalks, diced
3 cloves garlic
1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika
1 bay leaf
1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
8 cups vegetable stock
2 handfuls chopped spinach
juice of half a lemon
olive oil, salt, black pepper

method
soften the onion, carrot and celery in oil for 8 min with a pinch of salt. Don't brown them.
add garlic, cumin and paprika, stir 1 min.
add lentils, tomatoes, stock and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then simmer 35 to 40 min. Stir every 10 minutes so the bottom doesn't catch.
when the lentils are soft (not mushy), take out the bay leaf and stir in the spinach. It wilts in a minute.
lemon juice and salt until it tastes right. The lemon is the important part, it wakes everything up.

tips
Lentils soak up a lot of liquid. If it thickens too much in the fridge, add water when reheating.
Don't salt until the lentils are cooked or they can stay firm.
A parmesan rind simmered in the pot is good if you have one.

Grandpa Ike used to say it's not a real soup without a little vinegar at the end. I tried sherry vinegar last Tuesday and it's better. Write this down properly next time.

For freezing: portion into deli containers, leave an inch of headroom. Keeps 3 months.
