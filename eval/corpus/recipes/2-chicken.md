# lemon garlic thighs, sheet pan

Serves 4. 10 min active, 35 min in the oven.

Ingredients:
- 8 bone-in skin-on thighs (about 3 lb)
- 2 lemons, one sliced thin, one juiced
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp dried oregano, salt, pepper
- 1 cup halved cherry tomatoes
- 1 small red onion, cut in wedges

Steps:
1. Heat oven to 425F. Pat the thighs dry. This is the important part for crispy skin.
2. Mix lemon juice, oil, garlic, paprika, oregano and 1.5 tsp salt. Rub over the thighs, skin side especially.
3. Lay thighs skin up on a sheet pan. Scatter tomatoes, onion and lemon slices around them, not on top.
4. Roast 35 to 40 min until the skin is brown and the thickest part reads 175F.
5. Rest 5 min. Spoon the pan juices over before serving.

Serve with rice or crusty bread to soak up the juices. The two-year-old eats only the tomatoes. A green salad with feta on the side.

Notes:
- Last time I used boneless and they dried out in 25 min. Stick to bone-in.
- Don't crowd the pan. Use two sheets if needed and rotate them at the 20 min mark.
- Freezes okay, but the skin goes soft. Reheat in a 400 oven for 10 min instead of the microwave.

Leftovers: shred the meat for tacos on Wednesday with avocado and lime.
