coconut chicken curry (the one from the Thai-ish cookbook, adapted)

2 servings, or 4 with rice

what you need
- 1.5 lb boneless thighs, cut into bite pieces
- 1 can (13.5 oz) full fat coconut milk, shake the can first
- 2 tbsp red curry paste (Maesri works, Mae Ploy is too sweet)
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper
- 2 cloves garlic, 1 inch fresh ginger grated
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp palm sugar or brown sugar
- juice of 1 lime
- handful of thai basil or regular basil
- cooked jasmine rice to serve

what to do
heat a little oil in a wide pan. fry the curry paste 1 min until it smells toasty, then add the onion and cook 4 min.
add the chicken and brown the outside, about 5 min.
pour in about half the coconut milk and let it bubble. The oil will separate on top, that's what you want.
add the rest of the coconut milk, the pepper, garlic and ginger. simmer 12 min on medium low.
fish sauce and sugar, then taste. more lime if flat.
basil goes in off the heat.

notes
- Jess says it needs more heat. Add a sliced serrano at the start if you want it hotter.
- if the sauce looks greasy and broken at the end, the heat was too high. Turn it down and stir.
- don't use light coconut milk. It tastes thin and the chicken gets rubbery.
- leftovers are better the next day. Keep them in glass, the sauce stains plastic.
