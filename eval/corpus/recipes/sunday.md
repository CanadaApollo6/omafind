# Sunday lasagna (feeds 8, or 6 with leftovers guaranteed)

This is Nonna Carla's recipe, copied off the back of a Hoover box. Ask Dad before changing anything.

Sauce (make the day before if you can):
- 2 lb ground pork + 1 lb beef, browned in batches
- 1 onion finely diced, 3 cloves garlic
- 2 28 oz cans San Marzano crushed tomatoes
- 1 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tsp sugar, salt, dried basil
- simmer 90 min, uncovered

Bechamel:
- 6 tbsp butter, 6 tbsp flour, 4 cups whole milk warmed
- whisk until thick, nutmeg, salt, 1/2 cup parmesan

Assembly:
- 1 lb fresh pasta sheets from Fiore's on Fourth (or 12 oz no-boil dried, Barilla is fine)
- 15 oz ricotta mixed with 1 egg and a handful of parsley
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella, 1 cup grated parm
- layer: sauce, pasta, ricotta, meat, bechamel, mozzarella. Repeat three times, finish with sauce and parm

Bake 375F covered 35 min, uncovered 15 min. Rest 20 min before cutting or it falls apart. Dad always cuts it in the pan and says "the sauce is the boss," whatever that means.

Notes from last time: I used too much sauce and the middle was soup. About 3 cups per layer is plenty. Buy an extra pound of mozzarella next time; the kids ate the whole top corner before anyone could serve.

Shopping list: pork (ask Greg at the butcher), good ricotta (not the watery kind), basil, two cans of tomatoes, a parm wedge.
