outline - causes of the French Revolution (5 pages, due Nov 22)

Thesis draft: The Revolution of 1789 was not caused by one event. It came from a fiscal crisis that collided with an unequal society and a monarchy that could not decide whether to reform itself.

I. Introduction
- hook: bread prices in Paris in spring 1789. A loaf took nearly half a laborer's daily wage (find the exact figure in Hunt, p. 44)
- thesis as above

II. Financial crisis
- debt from the American war. Louis XVI spent about 1.3 billion livres helping the colonists. Make the irony point
- Calonne's proposals; the Assembly of Notables in 1787 refused to approve new taxes
- Necker recalled in 1788, and the report to the king leaked

III. Social structure: the three estates
- First and Second Estates paid almost no direct taxes
- Third Estate: about 97% of the population, paid the taille, the gabelle, and the tithe
- the cahiers de doleances, 1789, asked for tax equality

IV. Political breakdown
- Estates-General opened May 5, 1789 at Versailles
- dispute over voting by order or by head. The Third Estate declared itself the National Assembly on June 17
- Tennis Court Oath, June 20
- storming of the Bastille, July 14. Only seven prisoners inside, so the point is symbolic
- the Great Fear in the countryside, peasants burned feudal documents

V. Counterarguments to address
- Cobban argues the bourgeoisie led the revolt for economic reasons. Answer with Doyle
- the Enlightenment argument. Ideas mattered, but not as the main cause

Conclusion: the financial crisis forced the king to call the Estates. Everything after that was improvisation.

Sources: Hunt (Politics, Culture, and Class), Doyle (Oxford History), Mrs. Pell's handout on the cahiers. Still need one primary source. Try the Declaration of the Rights of Man.
