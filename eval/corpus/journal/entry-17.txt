The woman at the desk, Karen, said she was tied to a fence near the landfill in December and spent three weeks in foster, which is where she learned to walk on a leash.

Her name at the shelter was Lulu. We changed it in the car. Maya thinks the new name is because she's the color of a digestive biscuit, and she's probably right.
