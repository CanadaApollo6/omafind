jan 1, 2024

Woke up at 8, nobody else up. Made coffee and sat on the back steps in the cold. Not one resolution written down, a first in years.

2023 in short: the job change was the big one. I left the reporting team in March and I'm glad. The first two months were hard. I kept waiting for someone to tell me what I'd done wrong. Nobody did. I'm still not used to that.

Things that went well:
- running 4 days a week most of the year, got to 8 miles without stopping in October
- Dad's surgery went fine. He's walking the dog again
- four days on the coast with Sofia, just the two of us, no phone

Things that didn't:
- let the friendship with Jo drift. We texted twice all year. I need to call her
- the basement flooded in June and I put off the cleanup for three weeks
- spent too much on takeout. Numbers in another entry

What I want from 2024, loosely: less rushing. Read more in the evenings, not on the phone. See my brother more than once a year. Maybe go back to the pottery class if it's still on Tuesdays.

One concrete thing: fix the basement drain before the spring rains. Call Hal at the hardware store Monday.

I don't want a list of 12 goals that makes me feel bad about it in June. So this is it. Ask me again in June how it's going.
