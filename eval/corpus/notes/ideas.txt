garden plan - backyard beds (north bed, south bed, the tall one by the fence)

last frost around here: about May 5. check the 10-day forecast before transplanting anything.

Feb
- order seed packets (Johnny's catalog, still have leftover beans from 2023)
- start leeks indoors under the grow light, 10 weeks before transplant

March
- start tomatoes indoors (Sungold, Brandywine, Roma) around March 10, 6-8 weeks out
- peppers early March, they're slow. jalapeno and shishito

April
- peas and spinach direct sow April 1 in the south bed, they like cold
- onion sets go in mid April
- harden off seedlings: 1 hr outside the first day, add an hour every day for a week

May
- transplant tomatoes after May 10 (the 2023 cold snap hit May 8)
- basil with the tomatoes, marigolds along the edge to keep aphids off (Beth swears by it)
- zucchini and squash go in the tall bed. 3 plants max or the whole bed is gone

June-July
- water every other morning, deep, not sprinklers
- pinch tomato suckers weekly
- beans should come in by late June

August
- order garlic for October planting (Music and German Extra Hardy, ask Oakridge Farm)
- plant fall lettuce, kale and radish in the south bed after the peas finish

October
- garlic cloves in, 2 inches deep, mulch with straw

Notes from last year: the tall bed flooded in the April rains. raise it 4 more inches or put gravel at the base. squash powdery mildew started late July, buy the sulfur spray before then.
