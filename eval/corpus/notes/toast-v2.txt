Hannah, you put up with a lot. You put up with his playlists. You put up with the fourth-floor walkup in Denver. And you still said yes when he asked you to dance in the kitchen at the wrong time.

(pause for the laugh)

Let's raise our glasses. To Mateo and Hannah: may your Sunday mornings always be slow, your arguments always end in a joke, and may the van never end up in a ditch again.
