half marathon plan - 16 weeks (race: Lakefront Half, May 4)

Goal: finish under 2:05. Comfortable pace 9:30/mi, race pace 9:20.

Weekly shape: 4 runs. Mon rest, Tue easy 4 mi, Wed intervals, Thu easy 3 mi, Fri rest or cross-train, Sat easy, Sun long run.

Phase 1 (weeks 1-4, base)
W1: Tue 4 mi, Wed 4x400 at 1 mi pace, Thu 3 mi, Sat 4 mi, Sun 6 mi
W2: same shape, Sun 7 mi
W3: Sun 8 mi, add strides after the easy runs
W4 (recovery): Sun 6 mi, intervals drop to 3x400

Phase 2 (weeks 5-10, build)
W5: Sun 9 mi. Start tempo: 2 mi at 9:00/mi on Wed instead of intervals
W6: Sun 10 mi, tempo 3 mi
W7: Sun 11 mi, hill repeats Thu (6 x hills on the Gap bridge)
W8: Sun 12 mi, tempo 3 mi
W9: Sun 13 mi (longest), first gel test
W10: Sun 10 mi, recovery week

Phase 3 (weeks 11-14, peak)
W11: Sun 12 mi, tempo 4 mi
W12: Sun 14 mi, last 3 miles at race pace
W13: Sun 11 mi, taper starts. Tempo 2 mi
W14: Sun 8 mi, easy

Phase 4 (weeks 15-16, taper)
W15: 6 mi long run, 2 short runs, race-pace strides
W16: race week. Mon 3 mi, Wed 2 mi shakeout, Sat rest, Sun race

Fueling: gel every 45 min on runs over 90 min. Test the blue Maurten on the 12-miler. Long runs: 16 oz of water every 30 min.

Shoes: Brooks Ghost, retire pair #2 at 400 miles. It's at 310 now.

Knee: if the left knee pain lasts more than a week, see Dr. Kim (physio) before the next long run. Ice and foam roll after every long run.
